# 8. COMMON REFRACTIVE ERRORS

## Myopia

Myopia, commonly known as short-sightedness, can make distant objects appear blurred while nearby objects may remain clearer.

Common examples include difficulty:

* Reading a classroom board
* Seeing road signs
* Watching distant television
* Recognizing people from a distance

---

## Hyperopia

Hyperopia, or long-sightedness, can affect the ability to focus on nearby objects and may sometimes contribute to visual discomfort.

---

## Astigmatism

Astigmatism can cause blurred or distorted vision because the eye does not focus light evenly.

People may experience:

* Blurred vision
* Eye strain
* Headaches
* Difficulty seeing clearly at certain distances

---

## Presbyopia

Presbyopia is an age-related change that commonly affects near vision.

Typical symptoms include:

* Difficulty reading small text
* Holding books or phones farther away
* Eye strain during close work
* Needing brighter light for reading

Appropriate vision correction can be discussed following professional assessment.

# 20. CATARACT AWARENESS

## Understanding Cataracts

A cataract is a clouding of the eye's natural lens.

Common symptoms may include:

* Blurred vision
* Increased glare
* Difficulty seeing at night
* Faded colours
* Frequent changes in spectacle prescription

If cataract is suspected, patients should undergo appropriate professional evaluation.

> Whether cataract consultation or surgery is provided by Sri Sankara Nethralayam and Opticals should be confirmed before publishing as a clinic service.

---

# 21. GLAUCOMA AWARENESS

## Understanding Glaucoma

Glaucoma refers to a group of eye conditions that can damage the optic nerve.

Because some forms may progress without obvious early symptoms, regular eye examinations can be important, particularly for people with relevant risk factors.

Patients should seek professional medical advice regarding screening and treatment.

> Specific glaucoma diagnostic or treatment services at this center should be confirmed before publication.

# 32. EMERGENCY EYE INFORMATION

## When You Should Seek Urgent Eye Care

Some eye problems require prompt medical attention.

Seek urgent professional care for symptoms such as:

* Sudden loss of vision
* Severe eye pain
* Significant eye injury
* Chemical exposure
* Sudden major changes in vision
* Sudden flashes or floaters accompanied by vision changes
* Severe redness with pain
* Sudden double vision

For emergencies, patients should seek an appropriate emergency medical facility rather than relying on a routine optical appointment.
